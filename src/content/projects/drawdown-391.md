A low-poly, PS1-era first-person horror game, built solo and shipped under a
studio I set up for it: **Confuse a Cat Games**.

## The pitch

A young technician covers the night shift for the senior watchman at a
hydroelectric dam in the interior of Portugal, during a drought. The job is a
round of checks, the same every night — read the gauge, write it in the log,
walk to the next one. The senior watchman can't come in, so he talks you
through it over the walkie. Then the readings stop agreeing with each other.

## What it is

- **First-person, low-poly horror** in the PS1/PS2 visual tradition — chunky
  geometry, a retro post-process pass, flashlight and comms.
- **A real round loop** — instruments, a log sheet, doors and keys, a water
  level that moves.
- **Built in Godot 4**, with the building's shell generated from a Python
  script so rooms can be rearranged fast, then dressed by hand in Blender.

## The studio

Confuse a Cat Games is a one-person studio in Coimbra. Drawdown 391 is its
